# Why no front end

An ecologist can ask for the next review queue, missing field evidence or species register without opening a form designer. The commands operate on records and produce branded, read-only HTML snapshots and draft papers.

A screen is useful for mobile field entry, maps, photo capture, offline synchronisation and concurrent collaboration. This free base does not provide those functions. Its useful unit is the office review desk, supplied with exports or records entered through the CLI. Enterprise DNA can build and connect the required field workflow in your version.

There is no automatic species identification, wetland delineation, regulatory submission or statutory certification. Those remain professional work. The database is useful on its own for evidence checks, follow-up work and review history.
